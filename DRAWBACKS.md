# All Drawbacks

[Back to the README](README.md)

All 181 drawbacks available in the game. Random squares, ranks, piece types,
and move schedules are assigned when a game starts.

| Drawback | The catch |
|---|---|
| Absolution | When one of your non-bishop pieces captures, it must seek absolution by starting its turn adjacent to one of your bishops before it can capture again |

| Active Volcano | You can't move onto or orthogonally adjacent to a random volcanic square |
| Alternator | Alternate between pawn and non-pawn moves. Your first move may be either. |
| Always Check, It Might Be Mate | If you are checked, you lose |
| Atomic Bomb | If your opponent captures a piece adjacent to your king, you lose |
| Barbarian Rage | If you captured on your last move, you must capture if able |

| Bipartisanship | You cannot move left twice in a row or right twice in a row |
| Bishop Fan Club | You must promote to bishops. Your king and queen can only move diagonally |
| Blinded by the Sun | You can't end your turn attacking a random sun square |
| Bloodthirsty | After the first 3 turns, if you go 2 turns without capturing, you must capture on the third (or lose) |
| Boastful | You lose if you have fewer pieces than your opponent |
| Body Snatcher | If your opponent ever captures a non-pawn piece with an equivalent piece, e.g. captures a Knight with a Knight, you lose |
| Bongcloud | When your king is on the back rank, you can only move pawns and kings |
| Botez Gambit | At the start of turn 11, you must not have a queen, and your opponent must have a queen |
| Boxing with Shadows | When your opponent moves a piece, if you can move to the square they moved from, you must |
